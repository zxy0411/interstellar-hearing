## 程序效果 / Application-rendered effects

The original star scenery uses application-rendered stars and meteors rather than an external wallpaper. Added snow, petals, the moon, weather particles and water highlights are separate web effects. These effects are not claimed to be photographed content in the source assets. Daily voyage scenes are independent of home scenery.

原始星空使用程序绘制的星星与流星，没有外部壁纸素材。网页另加的雪、花瓣、月亮、天气粒子及水纹是独立效果，不冒充原照片或录像中的实拍内容。每日星旅保持独立场景。
